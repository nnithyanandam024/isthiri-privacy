Privacy Policy for Isthiri ThaTha

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Application: Isthiri ThaTha (Package: com.isthirithatha)

Welcome to Isthiri ThaTha ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, place bookings, or use our laundry delivery services, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, and delivery addresses.
  • Location Data:
    • Customer Location: Coarse and fine location coordinates to ensure accurate doorstep pickup and delivery of garments.
    • Delivery Partner Location: Foreground and background geolocation data from delivery partners while on active delivery tasks to provide live tracking on the customer map.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Garment quantities, processing preferences, timestamps, and payment method (Cash on Delivery upon doorstep handover).
  • Device Information & Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens to deliver real-time notifications about order status changes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To facilitate door-to-door garment pickup, steam ironing, and delivery.
  • To provide live order tracking and route navigation between customer addresses and laundry hubs.
  • To send status notifications (e.g., "Clothes Picked Up", "Ready for Delivery").
  • To process customer support queries and resolve complaints.
  • To improve application performance and verify system security.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party technology providers to power key application infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android application runtime and location services.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Push notification delivery.
  • Supabase: Secure cloud database storage, authentication, and real-time data streaming.

4. Data Security & Storage

Your personal data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption and stored in secure PostgreSQL databases guarded by Row Level Security (RLS) policies. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing companies.

5. User Data Deletion & Account Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, update inaccurate information, or request permanent deletion of your account and personal data. To request data deletion, visit our Account & Data Deletion Page, email us at isthirithatha11@gmail.com, or submit a request directly through the in-app Profile / Settings section.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the policy on this page and updating the effective date above.
