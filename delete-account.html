Isthiri ThaTha - Account & Data Deletion

At Isthiri ThaTha (Package: com.isthirithatha), you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time, without needing to reinstall or keep the mobile application.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

You can initiate an account deletion request using either of the following methods:

  1. Direct Email Request: Click the button below to email our support team.
  2. In-App Request: Open the Isthiri ThaTha app ➔ Go to Profile or Settings ➔ Tap Request Account Deletion.

Processing Timeline: Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 to 14 business days (and within a maximum period of 30 days as required by law). You will receive an email or SMS confirmation once the deletion is finalized.

2. What Data Is Permanently Deleted

Upon verifying your request, we immediately delete:

  • Profile Information: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Address Book: All saved pickup and delivery addresses, postal codes, and GPS location coordinates.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens and preferences.
  • Support Logs: Direct customer support chats and inquiries tied to your personal identity.

3. Order Records & Data Retention Disclosure

In accordance with applicable taxation, commercial, and financial regulations in India:

  • Transaction & Delivery Invoices: Historical completed order logs, dates, and amounts (Cash on Delivery receipts) are retained for up to 180 days solely for accounting, tax auditing, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Permanent Anonymization: Once the 180-day financial retention period expires, all order history is completely anonymized and all remaining customer identifiers are irreversibly purged.

4. Cancelling a Deletion Request

If you submitted a deletion request by mistake, you can cancel it within 48 hours by replying to your confirmation email or contacting us directly at isthirithatha11@gmail.com.
